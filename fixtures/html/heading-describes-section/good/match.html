Acme Router X2 manual

Factory reset

To reset the router to factory settings, hold the recessed button on the back panel for ten seconds until the status light blinks amber. All saved networks and passwords are erased.

When things go sideways

If the status light turns red, the router has lost its connection to your provider. Check that the cable is seated, then restart the modem and wait two minutes.
