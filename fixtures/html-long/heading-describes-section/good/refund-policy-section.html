What our refund policy covers

We get asked about this more than about anything else, so this page has been rewritten three times in two years, each time after a week in which the support queue filled up with the same question asked forty different ways. What follows is the current version. It is written for a customer reading it on a phone at the point of being annoyed, which is the only audience that matters for a page like this one, so it is deliberately short on qualifications and long on plain statements.

Anything unopened can go back within thirty days of delivery for the full amount paid, including what was paid for standard delivery on the way out. Opened items can go back within fourteen days if they are complete and in a condition we can sell again, and the amount refunded is the price paid less the outbound delivery charge.

Faulty goods sit outside all of that. If something arrives broken, or breaks within six months in a way that was not caused by use, we cover the return postage as well as the price, and there is no window on it beyond the statutory one. Send a photograph with the request and the queue will approve it without asking for the item back when the item is worth less than the postage.

Money goes back to the card that paid, usually within five working days of the return being scanned at our warehouse. Gift orders are refunded to the recipient as store credit unless the buyer asks otherwise, which is the one case where the money does not return the way it arrived.
