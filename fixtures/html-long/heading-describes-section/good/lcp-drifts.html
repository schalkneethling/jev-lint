Web performance notes

Measuring Largest Contentful Paint in the field

Lab numbers tell you what your page does on one machine on one network. Field numbers tell you what it does for the people who actually visit, and the two disagree often enough that only one of them is worth putting in a quarterly report. Collect LCP with the web-vitals library, which handles the parts of the specification that changed between browser versions so you do not have to.

Report at the seventy-fifth percentile, segmented by device class and by connection type. A median hides the quarter of your audience the number exists to protect, and an average hides them twice over, because the distribution has a long tail and averages follow tails. Send the value on visibility change rather than on unload, which fires unreliably on mobile browsers and loses exactly the sessions you most want to see.
