What our refund policy covers

We get asked about this more than about anything else, so this page has been rewritten three times in two years, each time after a week in which the support queue filled up with the same question asked forty different ways. What follows is the current version. It is written for a customer reading it on a phone at the point of being annoyed, which is the only audience that matters for a page like this one, so it is deliberately short on qualifications and long on plain statements.

Orders placed before three in the afternoon on a weekday leave the warehouse the same day. Anything after that goes out the following working day, and orders placed over a weekend are picked on Monday morning. The cut-off is the time the payment clears, not the time the basket was filled, which is why an order that sat unpaid overnight shows a later dispatch date than the customer expects.

Standard delivery within the country is two to four working days and is carried by the national postal service. Express is next working day for anything dispatched before the cut-off and is carried by a courier who will leave the parcel with a neighbour unless the customer has said otherwise at checkout. International orders go by air and clear customs in the destination country, which adds anywhere between two days and three weeks depending on the country and on nothing we can influence or predict.

Tracking numbers appear in the dispatch email and on the order page about an hour after the carrier scans the parcel for the first time. A tracking number that shows nothing usually means the parcel is still on a trolley in the warehouse rather than that anything has gone wrong.
